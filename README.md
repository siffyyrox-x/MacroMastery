# MacroMastery
https://siffyyrox-x.github.io/MacroMastery/

An interactive macroeconomics study guide I built during my Macroeconomics course to make learning more structured, visual, and engaging.

Instead of relying only on traditional notes and lecture slides, I turned the course material into an interactive revision system where concepts, formulas, graphs, common mistakes, and exam-focused topics could be studied in one place.

## Topics Covered

- GDP and Economic Growth
- Jobs and Unemployment
- Inflation and Price Levels
- Aggregate Demand and Aggregate Supply
- Economic Growth and Business Cycles
- Money and Banking
- Monetary Policy
- Fiscal Policy
- Government Spending and Taxes
- Core Macroeconomic Formulas
- Exam-focused Questions and Revision

## Features

- Interactive topic navigation
- Simplified explanations of difficult concepts
- Important formulas and calculation methods
- Common exam traps and mistakes
- Graph-based explanations
- Quick revision sections
- Structured chapter-by-chapter learning
- Dark, distraction-free interface

## Why I Built It

I created MacroMastery as part of my learning process during my Macroeconomics course.

The idea was to go beyond simply reading slides and memorizing definitions. I wanted to organize the entire course into something I could actively explore, revise, and use for exam preparation.

Building and using this system became an important part of my study process and helped me achieve an **A in the course and the highest marks in my section**.
